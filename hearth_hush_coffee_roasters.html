Our Story: Farm → Roast → Cup

Founded by two architecture and food-science graduates who wanted a calm, unpretentious café space with honest specialty coffee prices.

01 / DIRECT-TRADE HIGHLAND FARMS

Grown slowly under native shade canopies

We partner directly with smallholder cooperatives between 1,650m and 2,150m elevation across Sidama, Huila, and Cerrado. Paying 35% above Fair Trade minimums ensures selective hand-picking of only peak-ripeness cherries.

02 / SMALL-BATCH COPPER DRUM ROASTING

Gentle heat curves for natural sweetness

In our Library Quarter roastery, every 15kg batch is profiled by ear, sight, and digital bean-probe telemetry. We develop caramel sugars without scorching delicate floral aromatics—so your cup shines black or with oat milk.

03 / YOUR QUIET MORNING CUP

Crafted for deep focus & slow conversation

Whether you are dialing in a V60 at your dorm desk before an 8 AM lecture or settling into our acoustic-paneled study booths, every bag includes exact grind and water-ratio notes so café quality feels effortless at home.
